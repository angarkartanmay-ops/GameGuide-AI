# 60-second demo video: shot list

`assets/teaser-24s.mp4` is a slideshow of the gallery images, a stand-in. A
real screen recording of the product working converts far better. Record this
once and use it on PH (first gallery slot, via YouTube or Loom), Peerlist, X and
LinkedIn.

**Setup:** OBS or the Windows Game Bar (Win+Alt+R), 1920×1080, browser at 125%
zoom, a clean profile with no bookmarks bar, Black Ice theme. Ask the questions
once beforehand so you know what the answers will look like. Speed up the
waiting in editing (2–4×), and never leave a frozen screen for more than a
second. Captions on screen work better than voice-over, because most people
watch muted.

| Time | Shot | On-screen caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | Landing hero, game art cycling | **The game guide that won't spoil it.** |
| 4–10s | Click Press Start → type `/progress elden ring : beat margit` | **Tell it where you are.** |
| 10–24s | Ask "Who should I fight next, and what's a good strength build?" Let the answer stream in (sped up), scroll it | **It researches live: wikis, patch notes, the web.** |
| 24–30s | Click "3 live sources", then point at the "Hidden past: beat Margit" chip | **With sources. Nothing past where you are.** |
| 30–38s | Landing → Spoiler Shield demo: drag the slider from Limgrave to Liurnia and watch lines unlock | **Story answers stop at your line.** |
| 38–44s | Click Share → open the link in a new tab, spoiler still hidden | **Share it. Spoilers stay hidden.** |
| 44–52s | Discord: `/watch add game: Elden Ring` → the confirmation embed | **Same guide in Discord, plus patch-note alerts.** |
| 52–60s | Phone (or DevTools mobile view) with the answer laid out | **Free. No account. gameguide.online** |

**Avoid in the recording:** `/missables` and `/price` until the issues in
`README.md` → "Before Monday" are fixed. A cut-off answer or a "couldn't reach
CheapShark" message on camera does more harm than leaving the feature out.

**Export:** MP4, H.264, under 60s. Upload to YouTube as unlisted (PH takes a
YouTube link) and keep the MP4 for X and LinkedIn native upload.
