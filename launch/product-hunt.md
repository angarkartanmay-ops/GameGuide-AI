# Product Hunt: GameGuide

**When:** Tue–Thu of launch week, scheduled in advance. Launches reset at
12:01 AM Pacific, which is 12:31 PM IST. Be online from then until about midnight
IST for the first wave of comments.
**Weekend option:** a Saturday or Sunday launch faces fewer competitors. That's a
reasonable choice if your account has little history.
**Account:** use your real profile. In the days before launch, upvote and leave
genuine comments on a few launches a day. Self-hunting is fine.
**Never:** ask for upvotes in bulk, or join upvote-swap groups. Ask people to
*try it and comment*.

## Maker's first comment (this matters more than anything else on the page)

> Hey Product Hunt 👋 I'm [name], and I built GameGuide.
>
> **The problem:** I'd be [30 hours into a game], ask a chatbot one small
> question, and get the ending in the answer. Wikis spoil you in the sidebar,
> videos in the thumbnail. Nothing asks how far you've got.
>
> **What GameGuide does differently:**
> 🛡️ **Spoiler Shield.** Tell it where you are ("I just beat Margit") and story
> answers stop there. Anything past it sits behind a bar you can choose to click.
> 🔎 **Live research.** Every answer is read from wikis, official patch notes
> and the web when you ask, so it follows the current patch, with sources.
> 🧭 **/missables.** What you can still lose for good, from where you are.
> 💸 **/price.** The best PC price right now against the all-time low.
> 🔗 **Share links** that keep spoilers hidden for your friend too.
> 📡 **Discord bot.** The same guide in your server, plus patch-note and deal alerts.
>
> It's free on the web with no account. I've been building it solo since April,
> and it isn't perfect: if anything slips past the Shield, I want to know.
>
> **One question for you:** what's the game you most wish you'd gone into
> unspoiled?

## Launch-day checklist

- [ ] Page scheduled 3+ days ahead, with a preview checked on mobile
- [ ] Reply to every comment, fast, with specifics, never "thanks!" alone
- [ ] Share the PH link: X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp groups, college community. Say "would love your feedback", not "please upvote".
- [ ] Pin the PH link in your Discord bot's support server, if you have one
- [ ] Mid-day: post one update comment (e.g. "Fixed X someone reported this morning"). It shows you're listening.
- [ ] Next day: thank-you post on X/LinkedIn with numbers (visitors, questions asked, top games asked about)
