All four are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1:
https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

The files are the families as published, cut down for the web (the OFL allows
this): Geist, Geist Mono and Fraunces are the woff2 files Google Fonts serves,
split by unicode-range; archivo-latin.woff2 is Archivo with its variable axes
narrowed to weight 500-800 and width 100-125 and its glyphs reduced to Basic
Latin, Latin-1 and typographic punctuation. To regenerate it, see
scripts/fonts/trim-archivo.py.
