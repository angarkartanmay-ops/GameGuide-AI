## Matchups (14-day head-to-head voting)

Voters must open both products before voting, so the first screen decides it.
Above the fold, the landing page already shows the headline, the one-line pitch
and "Press Start". Two things to consider before Monday:

- **The live counter under the hero currently reads "12 players · 46 answers
  researched · 15 games".** Honest, but small numbers above the fold signal "nobody
  uses this" to a stranger comparing two products. Consider hiding it until the
  numbers grow, or rewording it to something that doesn't read as a total.
- Each matchup is a reason to post again: "GameGuide is up against X on
  LaunchBuff this week" is a natural, non-spammy share.
